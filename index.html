Research focus

Four lines of work

Medical education & assessment

Generalizability theory for exam reliability, peer-assessment rubrics for ACLS and ECG skills, very short answer questions, and student co-designed curricula.

BMC Med Educ · Med Educ Online · Adv Simul

Field epidemiology

Outbreak and death investigations across Thailand: a vaccine-related mumps cluster, an infant pertussis death, a road traffic crash, and digital influenza surveillance.

OSIR Journal · J Trop Med

Toxinology & snakebite

Russell's viper and cobra envenoming, linking venom pharmacokinetics in the lab with clinical outcomes and community-hospital snakebite care.

Toxins · Biomedicines · Trop Med Infect Dis

Cardiometabolic risk

Triglyceride-glucose index, HDL and predicted cardiovascular risk in people with type 2 diabetes and in Royal Thai Army personnel.

Sci Rep · BMC Cardiovasc Disord · Lipids Health Dis
